## 1. The source of authority

**`GD2/docs/source-comp.webp` (1436×4096) is the primary visual authority**
(`DNA3`). It is the client's origin document, it is a complete three-act descent,
and **no variant executes it.** Every deviation below is named with its reason.
Deviations that are not in §4 are not licensed.

**It is a scroll storyboard, and its authority is scoped to that.** It is
1436×4096 because it is **a journey rendered as one vertical image**, not a page
layout that happens to be tall. It was made to settle what happens to the visitor
as they travel down. Read it as movement; `DNA3` would otherwise let a reference
govern hierarchy and scale wholesale, and this document must not.

| The comp **governs** | The comp does **not** govern |
|---|---|
| **the descent itself** | typography — face, scale, weight, hierarchy |
| the order and identity of the three worlds | copy, and the exact strings |
| that it is **one continuous downward passage**, not stacked sections | the placement of any text block |
| the transitions between worlds, and where each hands over to the next | the type system, of any kind |
| the change in light and pressure on the way down | |
| **depth accumulating** — the sense that you are getting further in | |

**The tell that its type is draft-grade is in the document itself:** every row of
the compliance list reads `need longer name for visual balance`, and the footer
carries a literal `XXXXXXX` column heading. Type in that comp was **placed, not
set.** Treating its lettering as a decision to preserve is reading a storyboard as
a specification.

**`Inter Tight` and `Plex Mono` in the extracted design system stand. There is no
serif in this project.**

The comp is branded **Visitor Nexus**; GD2 is the rebrand. All comp copy reading
"Visitor Nexus" becomes "GD2". Nothing else about the comp's identity changes.

## 2. What revision 1 got wrong

Recorded because the error is instructive and cheap to repeat.

Revision 1 read the seven built variants and concluded that the landscape was
decoration — *a mountain is a height figure on a depth claim, the ocean exists to
get the depth back, and the metaphor never resolves*. It then made the record the
dominant and turned the page into an instrument.

**That finding is true of the builds and false of the source.** The comp resolves
the metaphor completely, and it does it in three acts:

| | The comp | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | A summit under stars. *"Audience is Everything. What is Yours?"* | Height. You can see a long way and you know nothing about who is on your site. |
| **2** | A shaft in rock — mossy walls, blue-green light rising from a flooded floor, **a figure on a line descending into it.** *"How … Will Grow Your Business"* | Depth. Someone goes down and brings the record back. |
| **3** | A floating island above cloud carrying a lit city, a road curving around its rim, cyan markers on individual buildings. *"Next Generation User Identification"* | Arrival. **The city is the data.** Every lit window is a record; the markers pick out the identified. |

You descend **from** the peak, **through** the depth, and **arrive at** the city.
The landscape is not decoration in the comp — it is the argument. `C16` — the
dominant is the subject, not the device — holds in the comp and fails only in the
builds, and revision 1 attributed the builds' failure to the source.

**The builds dropped act 3 entirely.** All seven. That is both the real loss and
the third act revision 1 correctly found missing — it did not need inventing, it
needed building.

So the instrument-led turn is overruled on evidence. **GD2 stays scene-led.**

Two devices from revision 1 die with it, and saying so is the result:

- **THE OPEN FILE is dead** — the live browser-observation plate. It put a data
  surface on the first screen where the comp puts a mountain and a question, so it
  competed for the governing event; it pre-empted act 2, where the record belongs;
  and the evidence runs against it — Alex called `index7`, the demonstration-led,
  scene-free page, *"very banal, boring."*
- **THE HANDOFF is dead** as the peak. It was `9 observed → 300+ matched` and it
  depended entirely on THE OPEN FILE. Without the observation it is a number
  changing.

**What that costs, plainly:** the page loses the only moment that was *proof*
rather than *claim*. Everything on this page is now assertion supported by client
logos and figures. The partial recovery is in §8 — the record resolving during the
descent is a demonstration of the *mechanism*, which is weaker than a
demonstration on the visitor, and it is what the comp supports.

```
  light, annotated as a document.

  full weight.
```

**Dimensionality is promoted from SUPPORT to MAIN** relative to revision 1, which
re-runs this Read as `TASTE.md` §2 requires. The scenes are no longer reinforcing
a composition that stands without them; they *are* it.

### What carries through untouched — named first, as `REDESIGN` requires

A carrier not named here is a carrier that will be quietly replaced.

1. **The `GD2` wordmark with the cyan numeral**, in its existing lockup, replacing
   the comp's Visitor Nexus pin lockup.
2. **Orange `#FF9300` is the promise; cyan `#00ECFF` is the instrument.** The

5. **The token layer** — `tokens.css` and the seven type ranks in

6. **The dark ground**, and the comp's specific light: teal-black sky, warm light

7. **Every line of copy, the nav, the figures and the phone number
   `(866) 591-7555`**, subject to the ledger in §11.

**Would a regular visitor recognise this as the same brand?** Yes — same wordmark,
same two-colour law with the same meanings, same three markers, same working face,

## 4. Deviations from the comp — named, with reasons

`DNA3` requires each one to be declared. There are four. **None of them is
typographic** — the comp does not govern type (§1).

### 4.1 The line attaches to things. It never floats.

**This is Alex's own distinction and it is the most useful note in the round.** He
likes the path on the mountain in `index3` and rejects it on the ocean in `index4`
— *"очень тупой и прямой, не интересный."*

The reason the two read differently: **a line that traces terrain is a route; the
same line over open space is a line someone drew.** The comp itself commits the
second error — its act-2 path is a straight vertical dashed rule down the centre of
the frame, attached to nothing.

So the comp is overruled here, on Alex's direction: **every segment of the line is
physically attached to something.** See §8.

### 4.2 The city's lights are cyan, not magenta

The comp's island city is lit magenta and purple. That is a third colour entering
the page at its most important moment, and it is also the single strongest tell of
generic fantasy concept art.

**The city is made of records, and a record is instrument, so the city takes
cyan.** Warm interior light stays for depth and habitation; the signal colour is
cyan. This obeys the project's own colour law rather than breaking it at the peak,
and it de-clichés the asset — see the dependency in §12.

### 4.3 Compliance and proof become one act with two registers

The comp has *Ethical and Compliant Data Storage* and *Customers utilizing …* as
two consecutive blocks. They make the same ask — **trust** — one after the other,
and a run of identical asks is a sales floor rather than an argument.

**Nothing is cut.** They compose as one mass with an internal register change:
compliance set as a legal document, proof set as names at rest. The change of
register is the pause, not the interval between them.

### 4.4 The figure is a cenote diver, and the environment is read as the comp draws it

The builds read act 2 as **open ocean**. The comp shows **a shaft in rock** — mossy
green walls, hard rock edges, light rising from a flooded floor. It is a cenote: a
vertical shaft with water at the bottom.

This matters because it is what makes the descent physically coherent. Open water
has no walls to descend past, no rope anchor, and no floor to arrive at, which is
exactly why the ocean builds needed a straight line down the middle and why that
line looked drawn. **The rock is what the descent is measured against.**

## 5. The concept, in one sentence

**You are standing on a summit knowing nothing about the people on your site, and
the page takes you down the shaft and puts you in the lit city that was already
there.**

It could be wrong: it assumes a dealer's obstacle is that the data feels
unimaginable, and that arrival is more persuasive than proof. If the real obstacle
is legal fear, act 4 would have to become act 1 and this concept is the wrong one.

## 6. The grammar

**Continuous world, in three chapters, with a hard stop.** Declared under `DNA36`,
and it is the comp's own grammar rather than a choice made over it — the comp is a
storyboard of one continuous downward passage, which is why acts 1–3 may not be
built as three sections that happen to follow each other.

Acts 1–3 are one continuous descent under one light — the visitor never leaves the
world. **Act 4 leaves it completely**: the scene ends, the ground goes flat, and
the page becomes a document. That cut is the page's one structural event, and it is
what stops a cinematic page from having to stay cinematic while it talks about
compliance.

## 7. The feeling curve

Emotion first, the thing on screen that causes it second.

| Act | The feeling | What on screen causes it |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · THE SUMMIT** | **Exposure** — *a question I cannot answer* | A vast cold sky, one warm-lit peak, one cyan marker on it, and *"What is Yours?"* with nothing on screen that answers it |
| **2 · THE DESCENT** | **Tension, then the first knowledge** | The rock walls close in and the figure gets smaller; against that, the record resolves one row at a time as it falls |
| **3 · THE ARRIVAL** | **Release into scale** | The shaft opens out into open sky and the city is simply *there*, entire and lit, every window a record — **the peak** |
| **4 · THE GROUND** | **Sobriety** | The scene stops. Flat field, no parallax, no motion: the compliance frame set as a legal document, then the names at rest |
| **5 · THE CLOSE** | **Decision** | One phone number, at display scale, alone |

exposure → tension → release → sobriety → decision. No two adjacent acts repeat a
feeling. Act 4's stillness is the silence that lets act 3 read as a peak.

**The two seams carry the feeling between the acts, and they are where the curve
actually bends.** S1 is *commitment* — the moment going down stops being optional.
S2 is *pressure*, the only stretch where the visitor does not know how much further
there is, and it is what makes act 3 read as release rather than as the next
section. Neither is a gap to be tuned out of the build; both are budgeted in §13
and shot in §8.

## 8. The shot list

**The feeling picks the shot; the shot picks the device.** Every device carries a
declared narrative role (`DNA35`, `MJ1`) and earns its advance (`MJ10`).

**The two seams are shots, not gaps.** They are the comp's real content: it is a
storyboard of a descent, and a descent is mostly the travelling. Measured, the two
seams occupy **14.2% of the whole comp — more than the summit world itself** (§13).
Every build treated them as joins and cut them to nothing, which is why five of the
seven hard-cut from mountain to water. The one transition Alex has praised in this
project is `index4`'s hero→ocean handover; it is the only seam anyone built.

Each seam is listed with what it changes (`DNA64`), because a seam that does not
say what changed is a dissolve applied everywhere, which is a defect rather than a
system.

| Act | **Shot** | Device, and the role it serves |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **WIDE, HELD, slow settle.** The comp's framing exactly: peak centre-right, headline left across the lower flank, air top-left. The camera does not travel; the *plates* do. | 2.5D parallax on four plates — stars / far ridge / lit peak / near rock — at differing rates. The summit marker breathes once and holds. **Role:** it establishes altitude, which is the only reason the descent means anything. |
| **S1** | **THE HANDOVER — over the lip.** The summit's air becomes the shaft's mouth: the near rock of act 1 rises and becomes the wall of act 2, so one continuous surface carries the visitor over the edge. No dissolve, no fade to black. | The plate that was act 1's foreground rock is act 2's upper wall — the same mass, re-lit. The route reaches the lip and becomes the rope's anchor. **Changes: ground and scale** (`DNA64`). **Role:** it is the only reason the page reads as one descent rather than two scenes stacked. **This is the transition Alex praised on `index4` and the only seam any build has made.** |
| **2** | **SUSTAINED DESCENT.** One continuous fall past rock. The walls converge toward the bottom of the act. | The line becomes the **rope**; the figure descends it, scroll-bound. The three capability labels dock to the rope on cyan ring markers as it passes them. Against the wall, the `SIGNAL → PROFILE` plate resolves row by row. **Role:** it makes depth *mean* knowledge instead of illustrating it. |
| **S2** | **THE FALL IN THE DARK — the long seam.** The walls run out of light and the frame goes to near-black with nothing in it. **This is the longest featureless passage in the comp and it is deliberate**: 190px of it are literally flat. In a storyboard a dark stretch is not an empty stretch, it is *duration*. | Nothing enters and nothing leaves. Ground and light travel continuously to black (`DNA65`); the rope keeps running through frame so the visitor knows they are still descending and the page has not broken. **Changes: pressure and light** (`DNA64`). **Role:** it is the only place the page lets depth accumulate as felt distance. Without it the city is a section below the shaft rather than something you arrive at. |
| **3** | **THE OPEN-OUT — the peak.** The only change of spatial category on the page: enclosure becomes sky. Cloud parts laterally; the island is revealed whole rather than assembled. | The rope's line arrives and becomes the **road** on the island rim. Building markers light in sequence, not all at once. Type unfolds rather than fades (Alex: *"текст динамичный, раскрывающийся"*). **Role:** it delivers the one thing the page cannot say in words — the scale of what was down there. |
| **4** | **FLAT FIELD. The scene is over.** Deliberately the plainest composition on the page — no parallax, no depth, no marker. | **None.** Entrances only, and they are off by the second half. Motion silence is the device. **Role:** it makes the peak survivable and lets legal read as legal. |
| **5** | **HOLD.** | None. **Role:** a close is a decision, not an effect. |

### Mobile is authored, not scaled (`DM10`, `MJ8`)

A separate shot list, not a set of breakpoints:

- **Act 1** — the peak moves to the lower third and the headline sits above it, so
  the headline is never crossed by the ridge. The mountain is re-cropped at source,
  not `object-fit`-ed.
- **Act 2** — the shaft is cropped to its lit core; the rope runs closer to the
  edge, and the field plate takes the full column beneath it rather than beside it.
  Six rows visible, three behind a `+ 3 MORE` line that exists in the static build.
- **Act 3** — the open-out cannot be lateral on one column. The island **rises into
  frame from below** instead, which is the same feeling from the opposite
  direction. Different shot, same release.
- **The seams survive on mobile at full length.** They are the first thing a
  narrow build is tempted to compress, and compressing them turns the descent back
  into stacked sections. If anything gives on mobile it is act 4, never S1 or S2.
- **Act 4/5** — identical to desktop in kind; only measure changes.

## 9. The peak — exactly one

### **THE ARRIVAL** (act 3)

The shaft opens out and the floating city is there, whole, lit, above cloud — with
the road that the line has been since the summit running around its rim.

It takes **the asset budget** (the island is the largest single asset on the page),
**the silence in front of it** (act 2's last half-viewport goes dark and quiet as
the walls converge), and **the most scroll room** (3.0 of 10.0 viewport-heights).

**Why this and not the record resolving:** the record is the *mechanism* and it
runs for a whole act. The arrival happens once, it is the largest compositional
event on the page, and it is the act every one of the seven builds dropped. It is
the payoff the descent is for; without it a descent is just a long way down.

## 10. The signature move — exactly one

### **THE LINE**

**One continuous line runs from the summit marker to the city, and it is never a
line floating in space. Every segment is attached to something physical.**

| Act | What the line is | What it is attached to |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | a **route** | traced along the actual ridge of the photographed peak — authored to that ridge, so it reads as a way down rather than a graphic |
| **2** | the **rope** | anchored at the shaft's lip, carrying the figure's weight; the capability markers dock to it |
| **3** | the **road** | the lit road curving around the island's rim, which the comp already draws |

**Why it is a signature move and not a parameter change** (`DNA37`): it is derived
from this comp and from Alex's own like/dislike split, and it cannot be lifted onto
another site — it only exists because this page descends past three different kinds
of physical thing. Describe it to someone who has seen all seven builds and they
will not confuse it: five had a dashed line floating over a landscape, one had a
straight rule down open water, one had no line at all.

**It replaces the ban.** Revision 1 would have thrown the path out; Alex's
distinction says keep it and give it something to hold onto.

**Static and reduced-motion:** the line is fully drawn, the figure sits at the
act's midpoint, and the record is resolved. A composed frame, not a disabled
animation (`DM4`, `MJ5`, `DNA39`).

### The record, inside act 2 — the thing Alex liked, fixed

`index5`'s `03 DESCENT · SIGNAL → PROFILE` plate is the only place in seven builds
where the product is shown working, and it is kept. Its fault was that every row
already read `MATCHED` before the visitor arrived — nothing resolved, which is why
the motion read as *"a miss."*

**Here the rows resolve against the figure's depth.** The deeper the descent, the
more of the record is matched. Keys are `NAME · PHONE · EMAIL · ADDRESS · INCOME ·
CREDIT`, plus the folded remainder. **States only — never invented names, numbers
or addresses, in any state including loading and error** (`CP7`).

`index7`'s sentence — **"The file already exists. You just haven't opened it."** —
is kept, and it sits as the single line above this plate, where it is literally
true. It is an addition, not a replacement: the comp's act-2 title stays.

### The figure — decided, not "to be made"

- **What it is:** a **cenote diver in silhouette on a line**, descending. Read from
  the comp: rock shaft, moss, water light from below. Not a swimmer in open ocean.
- **Scale:** 3–4% of viewport height — roughly 35–50px at 1440×900. Never larger.
  Its smallness against the rock is the whole point; it is the human scale that
  makes the shaft deep.
- **Detail:** silhouette only. No face, no gear read, no character. It is a
  measure, not a mascot.
- **Does it move:** **yes, in one dimension, scroll-bound only.** Its position on
  the rope *is* the visitor's position in act 2. No idle loop, no swing, no ambient
  drift. Stop scrolling and it stops — which is `MJ6` (the user keeps the
  transport) turned into an image, and it is what makes every stoppable frame a
  designed frame (`MJ4`).
- **Asset:** one silhouette with alpha, or an SVG. **Not a rig, not a sprite
  sequence.** `index`'s WebGL diver was a 3.3 MB rig and `index2` proved removing it
  lost nothing — the failure was that it was a 3D character, not that a figure is
  wrong. The comp's figure is a silhouette and costs kilobytes.

## 11. The WebGL question, re-decided against the comp

Revision 1 said *no WebGL* because the record was the dominant. That reason is
overruled. **The answer is still no WebGL, for a better reason.**

**All three scenes are lit paintings, and their value is atmosphere, not geometry.**
The comp's mountain is a warm-lit photographic peak against a teal star field; the
shaft is wet rock and volumetric light; the island is a night matte painting. Three
things follow:

1. **The project already ran this experiment and the photograph won.** `index` and
   `index2` ship a photographic hero that is visibly better lit than `index3` and
   `index4`'s terrain. After two 3D builds Alex's verdict is still *"almost right"*,
   with holes under the model. Two attempts have not reached the quality the first
   photograph had on day one.
2. **Both things Alex likes about the 3D builds are available without it.** *The
   path built on the mountain* is an SVG path authored onto the real ridge and
   drawn with DrawSVG — it traces the terrain because it was drawn on the terrain,
   and it looks better because the terrain is photographic. *The mountain scrolling
   under scroll* is a four-plate parallax stack, which `index6` already proved buys
   the same read for a fraction of the payload.
3. **`DM6` — one depth idea per view.** Parallax plus occlusion is the idea, in all
   three acts. A renderer would be a second one.

**What would change this answer:** if the visitor is ever meant to *move the
camera* — orbit the island, look around the shaft — that is input honesty and 3D
earns it immediately. Nobody has asked for that, and the comp does not imply it.

`three.js` stays vendored and unused. That is ~600 KB not shipped, which is most of
what act 3's asset costs.

## 12. The declared asset dependency, and the principal risk

`C18` requires a composition to declare what it depends on.

**This page's peak depends on one asset that cannot be re-shot: the floating island
city.** There is no ordinary photograph of a floating island. If it comes out
looking like stock AI fantasy art, the weakest image on the page is also its most
important moment — and *"floating island with a city on it"* is one of the most
AI-clichéd prompts in existence.

**This is the principal risk in this direction.** Mitigations, and they are
requirements rather than suggestions:

- **Graded to the same light as acts 1 and 2.** One moon, one direction, one
  atmosphere. The three scenes must look photographed on the same night.
- **Treated as night photography, not concept art** — long-lens compression,
  atmospheric haze with distance, real falloff. No rim-light glow, no lens flare,
  no volumetric god-rays.
- **Cyan signal, per §4.2.** Removing the magenta removes most of the tell.
- **`GI1`–`GI8` bind** if any part is generated: origin declared before generation,
  the prompt derived from this brief rather than typed from taste, no type or UI
  baked into the frame, the reading zones held **at generation** rather than
  patched with a scrim afterwards, provenance travelling with the file, and the
  **mobile frame authored, not cropped**.
- **The fallback, decided now:** if the island cannot be produced at that standard,
  the peak becomes the shaft opening into a lit *underwater* city read at distance
  through water — same arrival, same release, an asset class that photographs
  plausibly. It is a worse image and a safer one, and the choice is made on the
  render, not on the prompt.

## 13. The budgets

### Scroll — derived from the comp, not chosen

The comp's vertical proportions are **scroll budget evidence**: how much of the
4096px each world occupies is a statement about how long the visitor spends there.
Measured by row luminance and saturation across the full image:

| Region of the comp | px | share of the descent |
|---|---|---|
| Summit world | 0 – 510 | 18.1% |
| **S1 · handover over the lip** | 510 – 760 | **8.9%** |
| Shaft world | 760 – 1400 | 22.8% |
| **S2 · the fall in the dark** | 1400 – 1730 | **11.7%** |
| City world, approach to falloff | 1730 – 2810 | 38.4% |
| *(document ground: compliance → footer)* | 2810 – 4096 | *31.4% of the whole* |

Two things fall out of that, and the first is a correction to revision 2:

1. **The comp gives the descent-and-its-seams more travel than I budgeted.** Seam +
   shaft + seam is 43.4% of the descent — about 3.0 vh — and I had allocated 2.5 vh
   to the shaft with **nothing at all for the seams**. The comp is right: the
   descent is the idea, and it is the exact part every build cut short.
2. **The split between descent and document was already right.** The comp spends
   68.6% on acts 1–3 and 31.4% on everything after; the allocation below is 7.0 vh
   and 3.0 vh.

| Act | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · The Summit | 1.25 vh | 1.75 vh |
| **S1 · The Handover** | **0.6 vh** | **0.6 vh** |
| 2 · The Descent | 1.6 vh | 2.25 vh |
| **S2 · The Fall** | **0.8 vh** | **0.8 vh** |
| 3 · **The Arrival** | **2.75 vh** | **2.6 vh** |
| 4 · The Ground | 2.0 vh | 3.0 vh |
| 5 · The Close | 1.0 vh | 1.0 vh |
| **Page total** | **≤ 10.0 vh** | **≤ 12.0 vh** |

**An act may not borrow from the seam that follows it.** That rule exists because
the seams are the first thing a build under pressure deletes, and deleting them is
precisely how a continuous descent turns back into stacked sections. The peak still
holds the most travel of any single act.

For comparison: `index`/2/3/4/6 spend 6.0 vh on two acts, no seam between them and
no ending; `index5` spends 11.0 vh on five stacked chapters.

## 14. Content ledger — corrected, and still Alex's call

Revision 1 reported both of these as introduced by the builds. **Both originate in
the comp.** The builds inherited them faithfully, which is to their credit.

1. **`CDPR` is a hardened placeholder, not a typo.** In the comp it reads
   `CDPR ....... need longer name for visual balance`, and **every row of that list
   carries the same note** — `CCPA`, `FCRA`, `GLBA`, `DPPA` all followed by *"need
   longer name for visual"*. It is a layout annotation from the designer asking for
   longer strings. It then passed through four builds into body copy and into the
   footer legal line as a compliance claim on a compliance product.
   **This is the more useful finding, and it still needs Alex's decision:** what are
   the five frameworks actually meant to be? Until answered it renders as a visible
   placeholder (`CP4`), never as a plausible acronym.
2. **`300+` vs `600+` originates in the comp.** The hero says *"we can fully
   identify 300+ data points"*; act 3's body says *"identifies over 600 data
   points"*; the stats block says *"600 + Data Points Per User Tracked"*. They may
   be two genuine measures — identity attributes vs behavioural signals — but as
   written they read as one contradiction, and **the page states both within three
   screens of each other.**

Everything else — `600 Million + Consumer Profiles`, `100 Million + Daily Consumer
Records Updated`, `30 + Years Of Data`, the eleven client names, the phone number —
carries from the comp and needs a source line in the ledger before it renders. A
prior build is not a source (`CP3`). The comp's footer also carries a literal
`XXXXXXX` column heading, which is the same class of placeholder as `CDPR` and must
not be reproduced.

## 15. What the motion designer owns

This concept hands over the **narrative roles**; the motion owner writes the MOTION
READ and PLAN, the register, the duration scale, the easing and the choreography.

Fixed here and not open:

- **One primary temporal idea per viewport** (`MJ2`). Act 1: *settle*. Act 2:
  *descent*. Act 3: *opening out*. Act 4: *stillness*, and it is designed.
- **Every stoppable frame is a designed frame** (`MJ4`). This is the recorded
  failure of the closest vault references — `lapz-io`: *"the visitor can stop on
  very dark or visually incomplete frames"*; `morningstar-ventures`: *"the page can
  stop on long transitional frames."* It is also the measured failure of
  `index`/2/3/4/6, whose first four frames are the same frame three times over. **A
  scrubbed timeline with no composition at 40% is not finished.**
- **Comprehension never waits on choreography** (`MJ7`). Every claim readable at
  rest.
- **The static build stands alone** (`DNA39`, `G7`).
- **Reduced motion is a composed frame** (`DM4`) — line drawn, figure at midpoint,
  record resolved, island present.
- **Mobile choreography authored separately** (`DM10`), per §8.
- **`DM9` — no ambient motion inside a reading zone.** The parallax stops where the
  body copy sits, in all three acts.
- **The removal test on every device** (`MJ10`): take it out — if only the spectacle
  goes, it was carrying ambition, not content. The diver in `index` failed this
  test, which is what `index2` proved.

## 17. What this concept refuses

- **No WebGL, no canvas scene, no video.** §11.
- **No line that floats.** Every segment attached. §4.1.
- **No magenta at the peak.** §4.2.
- **No fabricated data in any state**, including skeletons and errors. `CP7`.
- **No fourth device.** Marker, `+`, rule. That is the vocabulary.
- **No third typeface.** Inter Tight and Plex Mono. A voice of assertion is bought
  with scale, placement and the accent word, never with a new family.
- **No act longer than 3.0 vh**, and none whose frames repeat.
- **No seam deleted, shortened or turned into a dissolve.** An act may not borrow
  from the seam that follows it. Deleting a seam is how a descent becomes stacked
  sections, and it is what five of the seven builds did.
- **No second signature move.** One page, one committed gesture.
- **No parallax under body copy.** `DM9`.
- **No new `indexN`.** The next artefact is `index.html` in `_TEST`. If it needs a
  variant to be judged, the concept was not finished — and that is a finding that
  comes back to this file.
