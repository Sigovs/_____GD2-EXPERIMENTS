**The closest reference in the vault is `ciridae-com` (rating 3)** — *"a disciplined
enterprise website that avoids the familiar visual language of AI startups… large
editorial typography, restrained metadata, deep navy fields, warm neutral surfaces,
numbered structures, and fine horizontal rules turn a technically complex platform
into a calm and credible system."* That is this brief in one sentence, and it is
the same project type: a complex instrument sold to people who have to believe it.

**Its recorded weaknesses are the traps, and they are load-bearing here:**
*"несколько секций используют почти одинаковую схему «большой headline + пояснение»"*
— so no two parts of this page share a form; *"местами визуальный ритм слишком

measured on the composited render; *"детали продукта приходится искать"* — so the
figures and the field list are in the open, not behind a link.

## The concept, in one sentence

**The file already exists.** The page is not a journey down a mountain — it is the
opening of a record that was written before the visitor arrived, and every part of
it is a page of that record.

## The peak

The field list resolving into a plate — nine fields, set as a specification table
rather than as a redaction that lifts. `index5` performs the reveal; this one
**publishes** it, because `ciridae-com`'s finding is that *numbered lines, tables
and fine rules turn technical information into a designed system*, and a table the
visitor can read at a glance is worth more to a dealer than a mechanism they have
to watch.

## E3 · the five conditions, as the acceptance test

Alex's own conditions for absence reading as intention rather than as failure. Every
screen of this page is checked against them before it ships:

1. a strong compositional support
2. an obvious focal point
3. a deliberate typographic hierarchy
4. a sufficient signal of continuation or completion
5. no impression of a broken load

Condition 4 is the one this project has failed before, and it is why every part
carries its number and its title in the same place: the visitor can always see that
there is a 03 after the 02.

## What the build changed, and why

| Changed | Why |
|---|---|
| The plate no longer buries the wordmark; it dissolves where it crosses it | The first pass put the ridge over the letterforms at full opacity and the mark was gone. `beings-co`'s move is that the portraits **interrupt** the mark and change how it is read — a mark that cannot be read has not been interrupted, it has been replaced. The plate now fades through the band the letterforms occupy, so both survive and neither is a layer over the other (`B2`). |
| The claim's second half lost its italic | Inter Tight ships four romans in this project and no italic, so `font-style: italic` asks the browser to slant a roman. A synthesised face is visible as one at 60px, and a display face is verified in the render, not assumed (typography `I10`). The half about the reader is marked by tone instead. |
| Three of the five cream figures were wrong, and one was illegal | They were written from an estimate on the first pass. Measured: ink 14.94 not 15.9, supporting 5.74 not 6.4, and the caption tone **3.96 not 4.6** — under the AA floor for the 14px numbers it sets. It is `#626676` now, at 4.71:1. |
| Colour steps out of the cream parts entirely | Orange on the cream measures **1.84:1** and cyan **1.20:1**. Neither survives the flip even as a mark, and a mark nobody can see is not restraint — it is an absent layer. Rank on those parts is carried by scale, weight and rule. The masthead's cyan numeral takes the ink there for the same reason. |

## Motion, and why there is so little

`C1e`: motion as identity is accepted only where the subject is temporal, and the
discriminator is the subject rather than the amount. index3 and index4 move because
a camera travels; index5 and index6 move because a visit descends. **Nothing on
this page is travelling** — it is a record being opened — so nothing here is
scrubbed, pinned or scroll-driven. What remains is an entrance and a bar that knows
which ground it is over, and both survive `MJ10`'s removal test: take them away and
the page loses its manners, not its argument. No GSAP is loaded.

## E3 · the five conditions, checked

1. **Compositional support** — every part opens on a rule carrying its number and title.
2. **An obvious focal point** — one per part: the mass, the plate, the four figures, the three columns, the number.
3. **A deliberate typographic hierarchy** — four ranks and no more, one voice for statements and one for metadata.
4. **A signal of continuation** — the numbering runs 01 → 05 and is visible in every part.
5. **No impression of a broken load** — the page is 5.1 screens with no stretch of empty ground; the reveals hold content for at most 0.7s and only ever below the fold.

No horizontal overflow at 390 or 1440. Nineteen reveal blocks, all of which resolve.
