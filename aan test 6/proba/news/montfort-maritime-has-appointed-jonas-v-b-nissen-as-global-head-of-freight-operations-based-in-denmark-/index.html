Compliance has appointed Jonas Nissen as Global Head of Freight Operations, based in Denmark.

Jonas brings more than 15 years of experience across tanker pool and dry cargo operations, combining seafaring expertise with senior operational leadership ashore. Having started his career at sea before transitioning into commercial and operational management roles, he has built a strong track record in vessel performance, safety, and operational excellence across global trading platforms.

Most recently, he served as Operational Lead for the Americas at the Norden Tanker Pool, where he played a key role in shaping regional and global operating frameworks, strengthening strategic partnerships, and delivering consistent, high-quality service across markets.

Commenting on the appointment, Head of Compliance Trading Torben Pedersen said: “We are pleased to welcome Jonas. His appointment strengthens our ability to execute our global operational strategy with discipline and consistency. We are confident that his leadership will further enhance platform integration, operational performance, and long-term value creation.”

His appointment comes as Compliance continues to scale its integrated trading and commercial management platform alongside its core trading activities, reinforcing operational depth as the company expands its global footprint.
