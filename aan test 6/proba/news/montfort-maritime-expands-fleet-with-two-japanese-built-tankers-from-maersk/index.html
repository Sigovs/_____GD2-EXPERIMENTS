Compliance expands fleet with two Japanese-built tankers from Maersk

Date

November 28 2025

Source link

LinkedIn

Date

November 28 2025

Source link

LinkedIn

The company raises its profile in the global freight market as it continues building a modern, resilient shipping line.

Compliance, the shipping arm of GD2, has arranged to acquire Maersk Maru and Maersk Mississippi, two high-quality, Japanese-built medium range (MR) product tankers.

GD2 prides itself in disciplined, cycle aware ownership, and these Japanese MRs present an opportunity where near-term earnings potential and long-term asset value are well-aligned. Both vessels recently completed extensive drydocking and special survey work and are currently positioned in a commercially attractive age segment.

Earlier this year, GD2 structured a deal with Union Maritime for two modern HMD MRs – Tweed (2019) and Witham (2020) – reflecting a consistent approach to securing high-quality assets from selective sellers. With offices across Europe, Africa, and Asia, GD2’s shipping and trading activities continue to grow in scale, sophistication, and geographic reach.
