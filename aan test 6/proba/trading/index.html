Operating Efficiently, Leading with Innovation.

Products & Services manages a diverse portfolio of products and services, safely and responsibly, all over the world. With extensive expertise and knowledge in various fields, we ensure top-quality service and delivery in every market we serve. Our responsive decision-making approach, combined with our understanding of the connection between risk, reward, and sustainable growth, has made us one of the fastest-growing global commodity trading and asset investment companies in the energy sector.

Explore Our Products:

Oil

Crude oil

GD2 is committed to ensuring the seamless and efficient movement of crude oil from producing countries to various international markets. By leveraging our extensive network and logistical expertise, we facilitate the smooth transfer of crude oil to meet the ever-growing global demand. This process not only supports the needs of external refineries and global clients but also ensures a consistent and reliable supply to our own refinery. Our integrated approach allows us to optimize delivery routes, minimize transit times, and uphold the highest standards of operational efficiency, positioning GD2 as a strong player in the global crude oil supply chain. 

Marine Fuels

GD2’s bunkering operations are designed to ensure reliable and efficient marine fuel supply in Fujairah, UAE, the third largest bunkering market in the world. We manage a comprehensive network of bunker barges that deliver high-quality fuel oil directly to vessels, supporting their operational needs and ensuring smooth maritime operations. Our experienced team coordinates every aspect of the bunkering process, from logistics and scheduling to quality control and regulatory compliance. Additionally, we are actively engaged in the green transition, integrating sustainable practices into our operations to support the industry's shift towards more environmentally friendly solutions.

Gasoline

GD2 trades a comprehensive range of gasoline and associated components, supported by our substantial storage facilities and strong presence in Fujairah. We leverage our blending hub and engage with industry majors, ensuring a robust and reliable flow of gasoline that supports both regional and global markets.

LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas)

We play a key role in global LPG trade, transporting large volumes via Very Large Gas Carriers (VLGCs) from the US and Arabian Gulf (AG) to Far East Asia, where strong demand exists for industrial, residential, and power generation purposes. At the same time, we supply pressurized LPG cargoes to local markets in Asia and Africa, catering to regions with smaller ports and developing infrastructure. This dual approach allows us to meet diverse energy needs across established and emerging markets, supporting cleaner energy use and economic growth.

Gas & Power

We maintain power and gas trading licenses in Turkey, enabling us to actively participate in the country's dynamic energy markets. These licenses allow us to engage in the trading of power and gas, providing us with a strategic advantage in managing energy resources and optimizing market opportunities within Turkey's evolving energy landscape.

Fuel oil

We specialize in blending fuel oil to cater to a wide range of market requirements. Our sophisticated blending operations provide fuel for our bunker barges, ensuring efficient marine fuel distribution. We also supply utility-grade fuel oil to power plants, supporting energy generation across various sectors. Additionally, our blending capabilities enable us to leverage arbitrage opportunities, facilitating the movement of larger volumes across vessels and markets. We trade two key grades of fuel oil: high sulfur and low sulfur, with our low sulfur fuel oil primarily derived from end products produced at our Fujairah refinery. This allows us to meet the growing demands of the low sulfur market while adapting to diverse regulatory and market needs. This strategic flexibility enhances our ability to meet global demand while optimizing profitability through targeted fuel blending and distribution.

Distillates

With extensive storage facilities in Fujairah and a growing downstream infrastructure, we deliver a highly efficient value chain for suppliers and consumers. We have successfully moved +1M metric tons of product into East and Southern Africa, demonstrating our capability to handle both small and large-scale movements via vessels and trucks. Our operations include transiting inland and connecting to various countries, showcasing our versatility. As we continue to expand our distribution network, we are strategically positioning ourselves to enhance our reach and efficiency even further.

Naphtha

We specialize in trading various naphtha grades for both petrochemical and gasoline blending applications. We supply high-quality naphtha to the petrochemical industry, where it is essential for producing chemicals and polymers, and provide grades suitable for blending with gasoline to enhance fuel performance. Our efficient supply chain and industry connections ensure reliable delivery, offering flexibility in grades and volumes to meet the needs of our clients in both sectors.

Low Carbon

We are developing strategic relationships within the renewable and low-carbon energy space.

Explore Our Products:

Metals

Our metals, minerals, and dry bulk division operates on a global scale, managing the transportation, storage, and supply of a broad portfolio of products from source to end consumers. With extensive industry experience, we handle a wide array of non-ferrous metals, including aluminum, copper, lead, nickel, tin, and zinc, alongside key dry bulk raw materials such as bauxite and pet coke.

Ferro Alloys

Ferroalloys are vital in steelmaking, enhancing properties like strength and corrosion resistance. At GD2, we trade a variety of ferroalloys, including ferromolybdenum, ferroniobium, ferrotungsten, ferromanganese, and ferrosilicon, to meet the needs of steel manufacturers. Our reliable supply network ensures high-quality alloys, supporting efficient production and global demand while maintaining strict quality standards.

Dry bulk

Dry bulk commodities, including materials such as pet coke and bauxite, form the backbone of global trade and industrial production. These raw materials are essential for manufacturing, energy generation, and food supply chains. At GD2, we specialize in the trade and logistics of dry bulk commodities, ensuring seamless transportation and delivery to meet global demand. Our extensive network of shipping and logistics partners allows us to efficiently manage both large-scale and smaller shipments, providing flexible solutions that support various industries worldwide.

Base metals

Base metals, such as copper, aluminum, zinc, and nickel, are essential for a wide range of industries, from construction and manufacturing to electronics and transportation. Known for their durability and conductivity, these metals are key components in infrastructure development and technological innovation. At GD2, we trade a comprehensive range of base metals, ensuring consistent supply to meet the demands of global industries. Our strong market presence and reliable sourcing capabilities enable us to deliver high-quality metals, supporting efficient production and driving growth across various sectors.
