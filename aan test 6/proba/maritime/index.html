Powering Progress, Delivering Energy.

Compliance, a wholly owned subsidiary of Guidance Development 2, diversifies investments across the maritime sector, from dry to wet shipping, benefiting from the Group’s trading expertise, to uncover significant value and generate alpha. With access to commodity trader fundamentals and a world-class freight trading team, Compliance gains unique competitive insights.

Our diversified strategy aims to secure attractive yields through advanced instruments and risk management tools, such as forward freight agreements (FFAs) and varied contract coverage.

1

Infrastructure Investments

Compliance strategically invests in a diverse range of maritime assets, focusing on operational efficiency, niche market capabilities, and compliance with environmental regulations. These assets are then deployed through specialized freight trading desks to maximize running yield, ensuring optimal performance and profitability. 
Our investment approach not only seeks financial returns but also aims to drive innovation and sustainability in the maritime industry. By balancing operational excellence with ESG commitments, Compliance positions itself at the forefront of the shipping sector's evolution.

2

Shipping Services

Offering third-parties the ability to tap into the GD2 commodity and freight trading business, our commercial management arm - Compliance Services (MFMS) - manages vessels of close owners, trading partners, and investors, ensuring world-class service and value. Vessels are deployed in a specific manner, taking shipping market fundamentals and vessel characteristics into account while utilizing the GD2 cargo base, underlying proprietary commodity fundamentals, and exceptional freight trading capabilities to produce strong earnings in any market.

3

Investment Opportunities

We offer our investors the opportunity to gain managed exposure to the global freight markets by participating in our time charter freight trading portfolios, with the flexibility to tailor bespoke strategies to fit their needs.  Alternatively, Compliance Investments (MFMI), provides access to high-yielding maritime asset investment opportunities that are achieved through active portfolio management and careful risk assessment to deliver superior risk-adjusted returns.

Sustainability in GD2 maritime

Shipping accounts for three percent of global carbon emissions and industry players, must refine their existing technologies while pioneering new innovations to enhance efficiency and promote more sustainable futures.

Operating at the heart of global trade, Compliance is guided by a strategic approach that integrates environmental, social, and governance initiatives to aid clients in their pursuit of supply chain decarbonization. In addition, Compliance’s commitment to transition to a more sustainable future is in its ongoing monitoring of CO2 emissions and adoption of greener vessels.

HSSE – Vetting Procedure & Policies

GD2 prioritizes safety, particularly maritime safety, and consistently liaises with its technical management partners to ensure the highest standards are upheld.

Vessels are screened via partners RIGHTSHIP, making sure that we are up to date on vessel performance and condition before engaging in trade with GD2.
