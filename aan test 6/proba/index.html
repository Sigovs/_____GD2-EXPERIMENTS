Ethical and Compliant Data Storage

Guidance Development 2 treats consumer privacy protection, and the security of the information we present and share with our clients, as a cornerstone of how we do business.

We operate inside the frameworks that govern consumer data in every market we serve — GDPR across the European Economic Area, CCPA in California, and FCRA, GLBA and DPPA in the United States.

Before a dealership is switched on, we verify the business, the permissible purpose it is claiming, and the people who will hold access. An account that cannot state a permissible purpose is not opened.

Records are encrypted in transit and at rest, segmented per client, and never resold. Every record carries its source and the date it was last refreshed.

We work with established compliance and identity-verification providers to audit our sources, our matching logic, and the chain of consent behind each profile.

Our processes follow the standards used across the consumer data and credit industries, and are re-examined on a fixed schedule rather than assumed to still hold.

Any consumer can see what is held on them, correct it, or opt out entirely. Opt-outs propagate across the network and are honoured on every downstream match.

THE REGULATIONS WE OPERATE UNDER

Consumer data is one of the most heavily regulated categories in the United States and Europe. These are the frameworks we build against, and the reason a dealership can put our output in front of its own legal team without flinching.

GDPR

The European rule on personal data. It sets the lawful basis for holding a record, the standard for consent, and the right of any resident of the European Economic Area to have their data erased.

CCPA

California’s consumer privacy act. It gives residents the right to know what has been collected on them, to have it deleted, and to opt out of its sale to third parties.

FCRA

The US Fair Credit Reporting Act. It governs permissible purpose — who may pull a consumer file, on what grounds, and what they are allowed to do with it afterwards.
